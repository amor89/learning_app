# Sampling

## Frame and quotas

The tracker samples adults aged 18 and over from an online panel. Each wave sets interlocking quotas on age band, gender and region, so the achieved sample matches the population on those three variables before any weighting. Quotas close when they reach 105% of target.

## Minimum base sizes

Never report a percentage on fewer than 50 respondents. Regional cuts need at least 100 respondents, because regional samples are clustered by panel source. Bases between 50 and 99 carry a low-base flag in every table and chart.

## Boosts

Wave W12 added a boost of 300 respondents in the North region. Boost respondents count towards the regional base but receive a design weight below 1, so the national total is not over-represented in the North.
