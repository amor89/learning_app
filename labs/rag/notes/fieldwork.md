# Fieldwork

## Field dates

Each wave runs for 10 days, starting on the first Monday of the month. Interviews completed after the close date are discarded, even if quotas are not full.

## Response rate

The response rate is completed interviews divided by invitations sent, excluding invitations that bounced. Wave W12 reached 23%. Panel response rates are not comparable with probability survey response rates.

## Quality checks

Remove respondents who finish in less than one third of the median interview length, or who give the same answer to every item in a grid of eight or more items. Log every removal with its reason code in the fieldwork run log.
