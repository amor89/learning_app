# Weighting

## Raking targets

Weights are produced by raking to population targets for age band, gender, region and household income. Targets come from the national statistics office and are refreshed every January.

## Weight caps

After raking, weights are trimmed at 5 and at 0.2, and the raking is repeated until the weights converge. Report the effective sample size next to every weighted base, because trimming lowers the design effect but never removes it.

## W14 change

From wave W14, household income was added as a raking variable, and the cap moved from 6 to 5. Totals for W14 and later are not directly comparable with W13 and earlier on income-related measures. Trend charts mark the break with a dashed line.

## Missing targets

When a respondent refuses the income question, the income margin is raked on the respondents who answered, and refusers keep the weight from the other margins. Never impute income for weighting.
