# Reporting

## Significance tests

Wave-on-wave changes are tested at the 95% level with the effective sample size, not the unweighted base. Charts mark a significant change with an arrow. Do not test more than ten measures per release without a correction for multiple comparisons.

## Rounding

Percentages are rounded to whole numbers in charts and to one decimal place in data tables. Rounded figures may not sum to 100.

## Release timing

Results are released to clients 15 working days after the close of fieldwork. The release note lists every methodology change since the previous wave.
